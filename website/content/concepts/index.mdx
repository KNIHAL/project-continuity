# Concepts

Three ideas explain how PCS works and why its output can be trusted.

<Cards>
  <Cards.Card title="Evidence model" href="/docs/concepts/evidence-model" />
  <Cards.Card title="Handoffs and resume" href="/docs/concepts/handoffs" />
  <Cards.Card title="Event log and integrity" href="/docs/concepts/event-log" />
</Cards>

Every fact PCS records carries a source (`verified`, `reported` or `inferred`). Handoffs are built from those facts, and the log they come from is hash-chained so that changes to the past can be detected.
